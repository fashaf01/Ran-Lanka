    Good to know

    All your questions, answered.

    How is the price worked out?

    Gold is priced by weight at the day’s rate. We weigh each piece in front of you, add the making charge and the value of any stones, and show you the sum before you buy. Prices on this site are estimates at a sample rate.

    What is a pawn?

    A pawn is eight grams of gold, the way gold has long been weighed in Sri Lanka. A one-pawn chain weighs eight grams.

    Is all your jewellery 22 karat?

    Yes. Every piece is 22 karat, 916 gold, with diamonds and coloured stones set by hand.

    Can you make a piece to my design or size?

    Yes. Bring a sketch, a photograph or a family heirloom. We draw it with you, weigh the gold in front of you and make it by hand.

    How does the viewing list work?

    Tap the bookmark on any piece to save it. Open your list from the top of the page, add your name, phone and a preferred day, and send it to us. We confirm every viewing by phone and have the pieces ready at the counter.

    When is the showroom open?

    Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 7.30 pm, at 192 Main Street, Negombo. We are closed on Sundays and Poya days.
