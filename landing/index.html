The 22 Karat standard

Every piece is 22 karat. Every time.

Pure 24 karat gold is too soft to wear every day. At 22 karat, gold keeps its deep colour and its value and is strong enough for a lifetime of wear.

916

916 parts pure gold in every 1,000. That number is what 22 karat means, and it is the standard for every piece in our showroom.

8g

One pawn. In Sri Lanka, gold is weighed and priced by the pawn. So is every piece here.

Our story

In 2008, Mr. Anas opened a small gold shop in Negombo town. By 2011 it had grown into a showroom on Main Street. The promise has stayed the same since the first day: honest gold, fair rates, and designs that never go out of fashion.
