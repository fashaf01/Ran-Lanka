Our story

2008

The gold of Lanka.

Ran is the Sinhala word for gold. In 2008 Mr. Anas opened a small gold shop in Negombo town, and by 2011 it had grown into the showroom on Main Street. The promise has stayed the same: honest gold, fair rates, and designs that never go out of fashion.
