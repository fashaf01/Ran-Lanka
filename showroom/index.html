Our story

රන් is the Sinhala word for gold. In 2008 Mr. Anas opened a small gold shop in Negombo town. Today the showroom on Main Street keeps the same promise: honest gold, fair rates, and designs that never go out of fashion.

  1. 2008

    The first shop

    A small gold shop in Negombo town.

  2. 2011

    Main Street

    A showroom with room for the whole collection.

  3. Today

    Gold and exchange

    Jewellery, bespoke pieces and money exchange.

Three things that have not changed

  • Honest gold

    Every piece is 22 karat, 916 parts gold in every 1,000, and weighed in front of you.

  • Fair rates

    Gold and exchange rates are checked each morning and posted at the counter. No commission on exchange.

  • Designs that last

    Necklaces, bangles and thali chains that families wear for a lifetime and pass on.

Good to know

          
How is gold weighed?
In pawn. One pawn is 8 grams, and every piece is weighed and priced that way.

          
How is the price of a piece worked out?
It starts with the weight of gold in the piece at that day’s rate. Ask at the counter for the full price of any piece, including stones and workmanship.

          
Do you buy old gold?
Yes. Bring it to the counter. We weigh it in front of you and tell you what we can offer before you decide. You can take the money, or have it remade into something you will wear.

          
Is every piece 22 karat?
Yes. Every piece in our showroom is 22 karat, 916 parts gold in every 1,000.

          
Why do the rates change every day?
They follow the world gold price and the rupee. We check them each morning and post them at the counter.

          
When are you closed?
On Sundays and on Poya days. We are open Monday to Saturday, 10:00 am to 7:30 pm.
