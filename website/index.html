Service · Sizing

Find your size at home

Your screen becomes a jeweller's gauge. Match it to a bank card once, then rest a ring or a bangle you already wear on the circle.

  1. Match a cardHold any bank card flat on the screen and slide until the outline traces its edge.
  2. Fit your ringRest it on the circle and slide until the circle sits just inside the band.

Screens differ, so treat this as a guide. We confirm every size at the counter.

This size is wider than your screen. Turn your phone sideways, or slide to the width you measured with a ruler.

The maison

Ran is the Sinhala word for gold.

Mr. Anas opened a small gold shop in Negombo town in 2008. By 2011 it had become the showroom on Main Street. The promise has not changed: honest gold, fair rates, and designs that last longer than fashion.
